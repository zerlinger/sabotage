THE 1982 ATARI GAME · RECREATED

SABOTAGE

Four rooms. Two lasers. One main computer.

Disable six control boxes to unlock the right wall.

✦

YOUR MISSION

The orange pixel is you. Shoot the control boxes; touching machinery or walls costs a life. Disable at least six control boxes to open the right edge. Reaching it too early transports you to the left. Regenerated boxes count as active again. Clear all ten boxes for a 5-point bonus.

STAY MOVING

Arrows / WASD move · Space fires · P pauses. One missile at a time, in your last movement direction. When the laser shields drop, dodge its aim or shoot its core for 5 points. Boxes stay disabled for 30 seconds in room 1, then 28, 26, and 24 seconds. In room 5, shoot through five layers of bricks and hit the central computer eight times. Top and bottom lasers fire alternately. Destroy the computer and escape right.
